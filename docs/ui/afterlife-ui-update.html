Why it looks like this

  
What shops in Pakistan run today

  
Checked on 24 November 2026. Each finding sets one design choice.

  

    

      
Nobody runs DOS at the counter

      
Pakistani desktops in October 2026: Windows 11 51.9%, Windows 10 47.9%, Windows 7 0.2%.

      
So the dead app appears only in the Studio, on a CRT. The shop gets a web app for the Windows PC it already has.

      
StatCounter

    

    

      
The old programs are Windows code nobody can change

      
Firms in Lahore and Karachi still hire developers to maintain and migrate Visual Basic 6 and MS Access programs. About 5,300 companies worldwide still run Visual FoxPro.

      
So Afterlife raises an app from its data files alone (Prompt 88). DBF works today; Access files are an optional extra in Prompt 85.

      
Rozee.pk · Rozee.pk · Enlyft

    

    

      
Owners already run the shop from a phone

      
Easy Khata, a phone ledger for shops, shows 5M+ downloads on Google Play. About half of Pakistan's web traffic comes from phones.

      
So the owner gets a phone view: today's sales, low stock, batches about to expire, and the last bill to send on WhatsApp.

      
Google Play · StatCounter

    

    

      
FBR wants every invoice online, and most are not yet

      
By the end of March 2026 only about a third of registered taxpayers were issuing live digital invoices. FBR lists 13,900 point-of-sale integrations covering 37,939 outlets up to 30 September 2026.

      
So every receipt carries the FBR invoice number and QR code, marked FBR-ready (sandbox) until a licensed integrator connects it. No FBR logo.

      
PK Revenue · FBR

    

    

      
Urdu on the bill

      
In July 2025 the Prime Minister directed FBR to launch digital invoicing in Urdu.

      
So the reborn app and its receipt switch to Urdu, right to left, in Nastaliq.

      
Profit

    

    

      
Our bet: keep the old keys

      
A cashier who has pressed F2 to save a bill for years should not have to hunt for a button. We have no survey for this. It is a design bet.

      
So the reborn app keeps every key the Possessor learned and prints it on the button. An app raised from data alone has no keys, so its buttons show none.
